Privacy at the little arcade

This note describes version 0.1 of The Wonder Ticket by Kasmer One LLC. Last updated November 7, 2026.

Your choices stay in the experience.

The reading is assembled in your browser or in the bundled iPhone app. This version has no account, AI service, advertising, analytics tracker, or server-side reading database. Selecting a door, feeling, and charm does not send those selections to us.

Saving is optional and local.

Nothing enters your ticket collection until you choose “Save my ticket.” Up to 20 tickets are stored in this browser or app’s local website data. They do not sync across devices. Remove tickets using My tickets → Delete. Clearing site data, uninstalling the app, or private browsing can remove your collection. Other people using the same device and browser profile may be able to view it.

Sharing is your decision.

Copying puts ticket text on your clipboard. Sharing or saving an image sends the content only to the destination you choose through your browser or device. Those destinations have their own privacy practices.

Web hosting and email.

Visiting the website necessarily sends connection details such as an IP address and page requests to the hosting provider. The provider may keep operational or security logs; this local prototype does not establish the deployed provider’s retention settings. Contacting us by email shares the information you include with us and our email provider.

Offline access.

The web version may cache its own pages and artwork on your device after a successful visit. The iPhone shell bundles those pages. No contacts, location, camera, microphone, or account access is requested.

Questions?

Email hello@kasmerone.com. We cannot recover a locally saved ticket you have deleted.
